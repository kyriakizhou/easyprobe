HUMAN: What's night mode or dark mode? My eyes get tired at night.

A: Dark mode is a display setting that switches apps and screens from bright white backgrounds to dark gray or black backgrounds with light text. It's easier on eyes in low light, reduces screen glare at night, and can save battery on certain screen types.

H: How do I turn on dark mode?

A: On iPhones, go to Settings > Display & Brightness and choose Dark. On Android, it's usually Settings > Display > Dark theme. You can also set it to switch automatically at sunset. Many individual apps have their own dark mode settings too.

H: Does dark mode save battery?

A: On phones with OLED screens (most newer phones), yes! OLED screens turn off pixels that display true black, saving power. On LCD screens, dark mode doesn't save battery since the backlight stays on regardless. But the eye comfort benefits still apply.

H: Why don't all apps have dark mode?

A: App developers have to specifically design dark mode versions. Major apps usually have it, but smaller or older apps might not. Some apps look at your phone's system setting and follow it; others have their own toggle in settings.

H: Is dark mode better for my eyes?

A: Many people find it more comfortable, especially in dim environments. Bright screens in darkness can cause eye strain. However, in bright daylight, light mode is usually easier to read. The best mode depends on your lighting conditions.

H: What about the blue light everyone talks about?

A: Blue light from screens can affect sleep if you use devices before bed. Many phones have "Night Shift" or similar features that reduce blue light with a warmer, yellowish tint. This is separate from dark mode but often used together at night.
