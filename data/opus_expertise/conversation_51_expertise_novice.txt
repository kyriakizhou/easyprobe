HUMAN: What are hashtags? I see them on social media but I don't really understand the point.

A: Hashtags are words or phrases preceded by the # symbol. They turn that word into a clickable link that shows all posts using the same hashtag. They're like labels that help people find content about specific topics. If you click #cooking, you'll see everyone's posts tagged with that.

H: Do I need to use them?

A: You don't have to, but they can help your posts get discovered by people interested in that topic. If you post a photo of your garden and add #gardening, gardening enthusiasts who browse that hashtag might find and engage with your post. It expands your reach beyond just your followers.

H: How many should I use?

A: It depends on the platform. On Instagram, many accounts use 10-30 hashtags. On Twitter/X, 1-3 is typically better - too many looks spammy. On LinkedIn, 3-5 is standard. Quality matters more than quantity though. Use hashtags that are actually relevant to your content.

H: Should I make up my own or use existing ones?

A: Both! Existing popular hashtags help you reach broader audiences. Custom hashtags work well for personal branding or events - like a unique tag for your wedding photos that guests can use. The most effective approach is mixing popular hashtags with more specific, niche ones.

H: I see some posts with really long hashtag phrases. Do those work?

A: Long, specific hashtags have smaller audiences but may be more targeted. #travel has millions of posts; #BaliBudgetTravelTips has far fewer but very specific users. A mix works well - broad hashtags for reach, specific ones for connecting with your niche audience.

H: Do hashtags work the same on all platforms?

A: Mostly, but with cultural differences. Instagram is most hashtag-heavy. Twitter uses them but prefers fewer. Facebook hashtags exist but aren't used as much. TikTok uses them heavily, especially for challenges. LinkedIn uses them professionally. Learn the norms for each platform you use.

H: What about those trending hashtags I see?

A: Trending hashtags are topics many people are posting about right now - breaking news, viral moments, or organized campaigns. Joining relevant trends can boost visibility, but don't force it. Using trending hashtags unrelated to your content comes across as desperate or spammy.

H: Are there hashtags I should avoid?

A: Avoid banned or flagged hashtags - platforms block some for containing spam or inappropriate content. Avoid extremely generic ones like #love or #happy where your post will be immediately buried. And never use hashtags related to tragedies to promote unrelated content - it's considered very poor taste.

H: This is helpful! I'll start using them more thoughtfully.

A: Good approach! Hashtags are a tool for discoverability. Used well, they can grow your audience. Used poorly, they can look spammy. Start with a few relevant ones and see what works for your content.
