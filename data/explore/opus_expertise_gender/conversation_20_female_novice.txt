### Human: Hi! I've been asked to plan my best friend's baby shower and I've never done anything like this before. I don't even know where to start. Help!

### Assistant: How lovely that you get to celebrate your best friend! Baby showers can seem overwhelming to plan, but they're really just a gathering with some special touches. First things first: do you know approximately when the baby is due and how many people might be invited?

### Human: She's due in about three months, and probably around 20-25 people? Mostly family and close friends.

### Assistant: Perfect! You have plenty of time. For 20-25 people, you could host at someone's home, a restaurant's private room, or a community space. Let's break it down into pieces: venue/date, guest list, invitations, food, decorations, activities/games, and gifts. It sounds like a lot, but each piece is manageable. What's your approximate budget - that will help guide decisions?

### Human: I'm not sure about budget. What do these things usually cost?

### Assistant: It varies widely! A simple home shower with homemade food and basic decorations could be done for $100-200. Catered food or a restaurant venue adds cost. Typically, expenses might include: invitations ($20-50, or free for digital), decorations ($30-75), food and drinks ($100-300), cake ($30-60), and games/prizes ($20-40). Often family members chip in or bring food dishes. You don't have to do everything yourself - delegating makes it easier and lets others contribute!

### Human: What about games? I've heard baby showers have games but I don't know any.

### Assistant: Baby shower games are usually simple and fun! Popular ones include: "Guess the baby food" (guests taste unmarked jars and guess flavors), "Baby word scramble" (unscramble baby-related words), "Don't say baby" (give everyone a pin - if someone catches you saying "baby," they take your pin), and "Guess mom's belly size" (cut ribbon to guess). You can find free printable games online. Two or three games is plenty - you want time for mingling, eating, and gift opening too.

### Human: This is so helpful! When should I send invitations?

### Assistant: Send invitations about 4-6 weeks before the shower. This gives guests time to plan and buy gifts. Include: the date, time, location, RSVP deadline (usually one week before), registry information (where mom-to-be is registered for gifts), and the theme if you have one. Digital invitations through Evite or Paperless Post are free and easy - you just enter email addresses and the site tracks RSVPs for you. Traditional paper invites are lovely too but cost more. Ask your friend for the guest list with contact info!
