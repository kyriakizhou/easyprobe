### Human: I've never really had a skincare routine. My face just looks tired all the time. Do guys even need skincare?

### Abstract: Absolutely - skin is skin regardless of gender. A basic skincare routine isn't about being fancy; it's just maintenance that makes a noticeable difference in how healthy and rested you look. The "tired" look often comes from dryness, dull skin, or under-eye issues - all addressable with simple steps. The good news is that effective skincare for most guys is just 2-3 products and takes about 60 seconds morning and night. What's your current routine, if any? Even just "I splash water on my face"?

### Human: Basically nothing. I use bar soap in the shower sometimes on my face. That's it.

### Abstract: Bar soap is actually part of the problem - it's too harsh for facial skin and strips away natural oils, which makes your skin look dull and can cause it to overcompensate by getting oily. Here's a dead simple starter routine that takes about a minute: Morning: face wash (gentle cleanser), moisturizer with SPF. Night: same face wash, moisturizer without SPF. That's it. Two products. The face wash cleans without stripping, the moisturizer hydrates and protects. SPF in the morning protects from sun damage, which is the #1 cause of premature aging. For products: CeraVe, Cetaphil, and Neutrogena all make affordable, no-nonsense options at any drugstore. A basic cleanser and moisturizer will run you maybe $15-20 total and last months. If you want to address the tired look more specifically, an eye cream can help with dark circles, and a vitamin C serum brightens overall skin tone - but start with the basics first.

### Human: SPF every day? Even if I'm not going to the beach?

### Abstract: Yes, even if you're just going to work or running errands. UV rays cause skin damage through windows, during cloudy days, and even during your commute. It's cumulative - all those small exposures add up over years. Sun damage is responsible for something like 80-90% of visible skin aging: wrinkles, dark spots, rough texture. This isn't about avoiding sunburn; it's about long-term skin health. The easy solution: just use a moisturizer with SPF built in so you're not adding an extra step. Something like CeraVe AM Facial Moisturizing Lotion has SPF 30 and goes on like normal moisturizer, not greasy sunscreen. Apply once in the morning after washing your face and you're done. If you do nothing else, this single habit will make more difference to your skin's appearance over the next 10-20 years than almost anything else. It's the one thing dermatologists universally recommend, regardless of age, gender, or skin type.
