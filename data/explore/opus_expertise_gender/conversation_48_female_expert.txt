How to address: premium grants are the cleanest approach - larger grant sizes compensate for illiquidity and risk through upside potential. Guaranteed minimum value is problematic - it breaks alignment by removing executive's downside exposure while preserving upside. Alternative to guaranteed minimum: floor price on options (discounted exercise price relative to current fair value) gives some embedded value while maintaining upside alignment. Another alternative: larger RSU component in the mix - RSUs have value as long as company has any value, providing some implicit floor while maintaining alignment above that floor.
