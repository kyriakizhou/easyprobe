### Assistant: DSMB with immunotherapy expertise is essential - immune-related adverse events require specialized judgment about manageability and expected patterns. Stopping rules for immunotherapy require nuance. Stopping rule framework: distinguish between stopping rules (hard limits that pause or terminate the trial) and review triggers (signals that prompt DSMB evaluation without mandating stopping). Stopping rules should address: unacceptable severe toxicity rate at a given dose level (for example, if 2 or more DLTs in 6 patients, stop that dose level); specific catastrophic events (death related to drug, specific severe toxicities like Grade 4 immune events not responsive to management); and unexpected toxicity patterns not predicted by mechanism.
