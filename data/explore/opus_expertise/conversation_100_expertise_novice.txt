HUMAN: What are apps and how are they different from websites?

A: Apps are programs installed on your phone or computer, while websites are accessed through a browser via the internet. Apps often work faster, can work offline sometimes, and integrate better with your device features. Websites are more accessible - no installation needed.

H: Why do companies make both apps and websites?

A: Each has advantages! Websites reach anyone with a browser without installation. Apps offer better performance, push notifications, camera/GPS access, and offline features. Many services have both - you choose based on how you prefer to use them.

H: When should I use an app versus a website?

A: Use apps for services you use daily - the convenience is worth it. Use websites for one-time or occasional use - why clutter your phone? Some things (like banking) are more secure in apps. For browsing or comparing options, websites are often more flexible.

H: Do apps take up a lot of space?

A: Varies widely! Simple apps might be 50MB. Games and social media can be gigabytes. Check app size before downloading in the app store. You can offload unused apps on iPhone to save space while keeping data. Android lets you clear app caches.

H: Are apps safer than websites?

A: Generally, apps from official stores are vetted for malware. They're sandboxed - limited in what they can access. But apps can request excessive permissions, and the vetting isn't perfect. Websites can be risky too, but you're not installing anything. Both require caution.

H: What about web apps and PWAs?

A: Web apps are websites that feel like apps - they can be added to your home screen and work somewhat like installed apps. PWAs (Progressive Web Apps) are an advanced version with more app-like features. They're a middle ground between websites and native apps.

H: How do I know if I should download an app or just use the website?

A: Ask: Will I use this frequently? Does the app offer important features the website doesn't? Do I have storage space? For social media or banking you use daily, apps make sense. For a store you buy from twice a year, the website is fine.

H: Can I delete apps and still keep my account?

A: Yes! Deleting an app removes it from your device but not your account on the service. Your data usually stays on their servers. You can reinstall anytime and log back in. Deleting an account is different and more permanent.

H: I have way too many apps installed.

A: Common problem! Audit your apps - delete ones you haven't used in months. Use website versions for rarely-used services. Your phone will have more storage, run faster, and be less cluttered. Most people only regularly use a handful of apps anyway.
