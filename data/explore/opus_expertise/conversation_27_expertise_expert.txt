HUMAN: We're seeing high garbage collection pauses in our Java service affecting tail latency. Current GC config is default G1GC. What tuning approach would you recommend?

A: G1GC tail latency issues often come from: too-large heap making collection pauses longer, mixed GC pauses collecting old regions, or humongous allocations. First: enable GC logging (-Xlog:gc*) to understand what's happening. Key metrics: pause times, pause frequency, old gen occupancy.

H: GC logs show mixed GC pauses of 200-500ms. Our SLA requires p99 under 100ms.

A: Mixed GC pauses are collecting old gen regions. Strategies: reduce old gen occupancy (more frequent young GCs, smaller heap), tune MaxGCPauseMillis (but it's a goal, not guarantee), or switch to ZGC/Shenandoah which have sub-ms pauses regardless of heap size.

H: We're on JDK 11. Is ZGC production-ready?

A: ZGC is production-ready from JDK 15+, experimental in 11. Shenandoah is also excellent for low-pause. Both achieve <10ms pauses even with 100GB+ heaps. Trade-off: slightly higher CPU usage and memory overhead. For latency-sensitive workloads, usually worth it.

H: If we stick with G1, what's the tuning approach?

A: -XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=100 tells G1 your target (it tries to adapt). -XX:G1HeapRegionSize affects how work is split. -XX:InitiatingHeapOccupancyPercent controls when concurrent marking starts (lower = earlier, more predictable). -XX:G1MixedGCCountTarget spreads old gen work across more pauses.

H: I see "humongous allocation" in logs. What's that about?

A: Objects larger than half a G1 region are "humongous" and handled specially - they can cause fragmentation and longer pauses. Identify what's allocating these (profiler or -XX:+PrintGCDetails). Often it's large arrays or serialization buffers. Pool large allocations or increase G1HeapRegionSize.

H: What about allocating pressure? We create a lot of short-lived objects.

A: High allocation rate means frequent young GC. If young gen is appropriately sized, short-lived objects die quickly (good). If they're promoted to old gen prematurely, that's bad. Check promotion rate. Adjust -XX:NewSize/-XX:MaxNewSize or -XX:G1NewSizePercent. Object pooling for hot allocations.
