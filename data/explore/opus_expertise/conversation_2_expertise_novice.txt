HUMAN: I want to start cooking more but I always burn everything. Even rice comes out wrong. Help!

A: Don't worry - cooking takes practice and everyone burns things when they're starting out! Rice is actually trickier than people think. What kind of rice are you trying to cook, and how have you been making it?

H: Just regular white rice from the store. I put it in water and boil it but it's always mushy or stuck to the bottom.

A: Ah, I see the issue! The key to rice is using the right amount of water and controlling the heat. For white rice, use a ratio of 1 cup of rice to about 1.5 to 2 cups of water. Also, once it starts boiling, you need to turn the heat way down to low and cover it with a lid.

H: I've been leaving it on high the whole time...

A: That explains it! Here's the simple method: Put the rice and water in a pot, bring it to a boil on high heat, then immediately turn the heat to the lowest setting and put a lid on. Let it cook for about 18 minutes without lifting the lid. Then turn off the heat and let it sit for 5 more minutes before opening.

H: Why can't I look at it while it's cooking?

A: Great question! The steam inside the pot is what cooks the rice. Every time you lift the lid, steam escapes and the rice won't cook properly. Trust the timer and resist the urge to peek!

H: Okay, I'm going to try this tonight. Thank you!

A: Good luck! And don't be discouraged if it takes a few tries to get perfect. Even experienced cooks had to learn these basics. You've got this!
