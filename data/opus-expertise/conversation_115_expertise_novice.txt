HUMAN: What's a hashtag for? I see them on social media but I don't get it.

A: A hashtag is a word or phrase with # in front of it, like #travel or #cooking. It's a way to categorize posts so people can find content on specific topics. When you click a hashtag, you see all posts that used that same tag - it's like a searchable label.

H: So it helps people find my posts?

A: Exactly! If you post a photo of your garden with #gardening, anyone searching that hashtag might discover your post. It connects your content to a bigger conversation. Popular hashtags can have millions of posts, while niche ones are more specific.

H: How many hashtags should I use?

A: It depends on the platform. On Instagram, people often use 5-15 hashtags to increase visibility. On Twitter/X, 1-3 is typical since space is limited. On LinkedIn, 3-5 is common. Too many hashtags can look spammy, but a few relevant ones help people find you.

H: Why do some hashtags trend?

A: When lots of people use the same hashtag in a short time, it trends - showing that topic is currently popular. Trending hashtags appear in special sections, bringing even more attention. This can happen organically or be driven by events, news, or viral moments.

H: Can I make up my own hashtag?

A: Absolutely! Just type # followed by any word or phrase without spaces. Brands create unique hashtags for campaigns, couples use them for weddings, and anyone can start one. If others start using it too, you've created a shared conversation.

H: What about those really long hashtags I see sometimes?

A: Long hashtags like #ThrowbackThursday or #SupportSmallBusiness are popular for specific themes or movements. They still work the same way - connecting related posts. Some people also use long hashtags humorously or to add commentary to their posts.
