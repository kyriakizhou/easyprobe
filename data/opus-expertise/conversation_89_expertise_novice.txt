HUMAN: What is USB-C and why are there so many different charging cables?

A: USB-C is the newest, most versatile connector type - it's the oval-shaped one that plugs in either way. Different cables exist because technology evolved: USB-A (rectangular), Micro-USB, Lightning (Apple), and now USB-C. Manufacturers gradually adopted new standards, creating variety.

H: Why can't everyone just use one cable?

A: They're getting there! USB-C is becoming universal. The EU now requires USB-C on phones, and even Apple switched iPhones to it. In a few years, USB-C will likely be standard for most devices. The transition period is frustrating, but consolidation is happening.

H: What's the difference between USB types?

A: USB-A is the rectangular one you've used for years. Micro-USB is small, common on older Android phones and devices. Lightning is Apple's proprietary connector (iPhones until recently). USB-C is the reversible oval, newest and most capable - handles faster charging and data transfer.

H: Why do some cables charge faster than others?

A: Cables have different specs! Some support higher power delivery. A cheap cable might charge slowly even on a fast charger because it can't handle the power. Look for cables rated for the wattage your phone supports. Quality cables from reputable brands are worth the small extra cost.

H: Can I use any USB-C cable for any device?

A: Mostly yes for charging, but speeds vary. A basic USB-C cable charges anything USB-C but might not support fast charging or fast data transfer. For maximum speed, use cables rated for your device's capabilities. Using a cable with an iPhone that came with a MacBook is fine, for instance.

H: What about power adapters/chargers?

A: The charger (brick) determines maximum power available. Your phone only takes what it can handle - you won't damage it using a higher-wattage charger. But a low-wattage charger won't fast-charge even if your phone and cable support it. Higher wattage enables faster charging.

H: Is wireless charging better than cables?

A: It's more convenient but slower than wired fast charging. Wireless is great for topping off overnight or at a desk. But a 5-minute cable charge gives more than 15 minutes of wireless. Both are useful for different situations. Wireless chargers are also not universal across all phones.

H: How do I know which cable or charger to buy?

A: Check what connector your device uses. For charging speed, match the wattage your device supports. Buy from reputable brands - Amazon Basics, Anker, and Apple/Samsung are reliable. Avoid dollar store electronics for expensive devices.

H: I wish I had known all this before accumulating a drawer full of cables!

A: Everyone has that drawer! The good news is USB-C is becoming universal, so future you will need fewer cables. For now, label or organize cables by type, test which ones charge fast, and recycle the obsolete ones. The cable chaos is temporary.
