HUMAN: How do I implement proper circuit breaker pattern for external services?

A: Circuit breaker prevents cascading failures when external services are down. Three states: closed (normal), open (failing fast), half-open (testing recovery). Track failure rate, trip when threshold exceeded, recover automatically.

H: How does the circuit breaker work?

A: Closed: requests go through, track success/failure. When failure rate exceeds threshold, trip to open. Open: immediately reject requests without trying, return fallback or error. After timeout, move to half-open. Half-open: allow limited requests, if successful close circuit, if fail stay open.

H: What thresholds should I use?

A: Depends on service characteristics. Common: trip when 50% of requests fail in last 30 seconds with minimum 10 requests. Recovery timeout: 30-60 seconds. Adjust based on normal failure rate and how quickly services typically recover.

H: How do I implement it in Node.js?

A: Libraries like opossum or cockatiel. Wrap external calls: const breaker = new CircuitBreaker(callExternalAPI, options). breaker.fire(args) makes the call if circuit allows. Configure fallback for when circuit is open.

H: What should the fallback behavior be?

A: Depends on the feature. Return cached data, default values, or graceful degradation. For critical operations: queue for retry later. For non-critical: skip the operation. Always inform the user appropriately.

H: How do I monitor circuit breaker state?

A: Log state changes (open/close events). Metrics: trip count, time in open state, fallback usage. Alert on circuits that trip frequently or stay open. Dashboard showing current state of all circuit breakers.
